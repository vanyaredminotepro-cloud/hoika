# OWW Phase 2 scope

This phase is constrained to **November 21, 2023 -> December 27, 2023**.

Integrated arcs:
- Mass mobilizations (OBKA/KK-9 and neighbors)
- OBKA-OBS border crisis and temporary de-escalation
- Great Novraniya / NNR insurgent arc
- NRD separation and escalation against OBS
- RNK-4 and drone-missile saturation period
- Disease arc (Mayaks-20), border closures and cure production
- Late-December coalition and regime transitions
