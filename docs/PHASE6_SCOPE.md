# PHASE VI scope (20 Feb — 13 Mar 2024)

This phase models the escalation from post-February stabilization into renewed bloc warfare and political shock cycles:

- Emergency military escalation around Filipania, Serlandia, Novraniya/Rhein and SS-Entor.
- OOS court/legal turbulence and leadership legitimacy shocks.
- Expansion and reversals of SSES/ASES alignments.
- Kubrill economic return, reconstruction, and rear-area logistics events.
- Renewed regional wars by early March with fragmented fronts and temporary pacts.
